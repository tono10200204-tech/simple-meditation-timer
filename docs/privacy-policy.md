# Privacy Policy

**Simple Meditation Timer** ("the app") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data collection

The app has no network access and no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not know who you are and cannot know, since it never talks to the internet.

## Data storage

The only data the app keeps is your lifetime meditation total, stored solely on your device using local storage (Android Room database). This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not backed up to any cloud service by the app
- Is deleted when you uninstall the app

## Permissions

The app requests one permission, `WAKE_LOCK`, which keeps the screen timer running while your phone is face down during a sit. This permission does not grant access to any personal data.

## Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect data.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the update will be posted at this same URL.
