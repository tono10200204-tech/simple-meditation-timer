## full description(4000字以内)

```
A simple, distraction-free meditation timer.

Tap the figure to start. A bell marks the start and the end of a sit,
and the timer keeps going if you want to stay seated past it.

- Tap to start, tap to pause, tap to resume
- 15 / 30 / 60 / 120 minute presets
- A lifetime total on the home screen, editable by tapping it
- No account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads, no subscription

Everything stays on your device. There is nothing to sign up for,
nothing syncing in the background, and nothing to configure before
you sit down.
```
